In short

Fashion creators in our network cover styling, ethnic wear, fashion design and lifestyle. Brands use them for launches, lookbooks, try-ons and UGC-style content. We shortlist on audience fit, agree deliverables in writing and report honestly.

What brands use fashion creators for

  • Product launches and new collections
  • Try-on and styling Reels
  • Festive and seasonal campaigns
  • UGC-style content for ads and product pages

What to check before you choose

  1. Audience fit: city, age and interests match your customer
  2. Engagement quality: genuine comments, not generic emojis
  3. Past sponsored posts: do they perform like normal posts?
  4. Disclosure: sponsored posts carry a clear label as per ASCI guidelines

The full checklist is in our guide to choosing micro-influencers.

How we work

  • One point of contact for the whole campaign

Frequently asked questions

How do I find fashion influencers in India?

Search by niche, city and audience fit rather than follower count alone. Our network includes fashion, ethnic wear and design creators. Share your brief and we will shortlist the best fits.

Which tier of fashion creator should I choose?

It depends on your goal and budget. Nano and micro creators often give better engagement per rupee, and macro creators give wider reach. See our guide on nano vs micro influencers.

Can fashion creators make UGC for my ads?

Yes. Agree usage rights for paid ads in writing before the creator starts.
