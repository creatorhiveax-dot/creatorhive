In short

Lifestyle creators in our network share everyday content across fashion, family, travel and wellness. They suit D2C and consumer brands that want trusted, relatable reach. We shortlist on audience fit, put the terms in writing and report honestly.

What brands use lifestyle creators for

  • Everyday product use and reviews
  • Launches for D2C and consumer brands
  • Event and experience coverage
  • Content you can reuse in ads

What to check before you choose

  1. Audience fit: city, age and interests match your customer
  2. Engagement quality: genuine comments, not generic emojis
  3. Past sponsored posts: do they perform like normal posts?
  4. Disclosure: sponsored posts carry a clear label as per ASCI guidelines

The full checklist is in our guide to choosing micro-influencers.

Frequently asked questions

What is a lifestyle influencer?

A lifestyle influencer shares everyday life content across topics such as fashion, family, travel, food and wellness, so the audience is usually broad.

Are lifestyle creators good for D2C brands?

Often yes, because their audiences trust their everyday recommendations. See our guide to influencer marketing for D2C brands in India.

How do I choose between lifestyle creators?

Check audience city and age, comment quality and past sponsored posts, not only follower count.
