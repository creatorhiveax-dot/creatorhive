In short

Travel creators in our network cover destinations, journeys and travel lifestyle. Brands use them for hotels and stays, travel apps, gear and tourism campaigns. We shortlist on audience fit and agree deliverables, timelines and usage rights in writing.

What brands use travel creators for

  • Destination and stay features
  • Travel app and product promotions
  • Gear and accessories
  • Tourism and experience campaigns

What to check before you choose

  1. Audience fit: city, age and interests match your customer
  2. Engagement quality: genuine comments, not generic emojis
  3. Past sponsored posts: do they perform like normal posts?
  4. Disclosure: sponsored posts carry a clear label as per ASCI guidelines

The full checklist is in our guide to choosing micro-influencers.

Frequently asked questions

How do I work with travel influencers in India?

Share your brief, destination and dates. We shortlist travel creators whose audience matches your customer and agree deliverables in writing.

Do travel creators need advance notice?

Usually yes, since trips and shoots take planning. Agree timelines in the written agreement.

Who covers travel costs?

This is agreed up front in writing, together with deliverables and fees.
