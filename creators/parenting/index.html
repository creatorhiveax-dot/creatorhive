In short

Parenting and mom creators in our network share family life, toddler tips and everyday routines. They suit baby, kids, family and home brands. We shortlist on audience fit, agree terms in writing and make sure children's content is handled with care.

What brands use parenting creators for

  • Baby and kids products
  • Family and home brands
  • Everyday routines and hacks
  • Honest reviews from real parents

What to check before you choose

  1. Audience fit: city, age and interests match your customer
  2. Engagement quality: genuine comments, not generic emojis
  3. Past sponsored posts: do they perform like normal posts?
  4. Disclosure: sponsored posts carry a clear label as per ASCI guidelines

The full checklist is in our guide to choosing micro-influencers.

Frequently asked questions

Who should work with parenting influencers?

Brands selling to parents and families, such as baby care, kids products, home and food.

How do I check a parenting creator is a good fit?

Look at audience city, age and engagement, and whether their tone feels honest and relatable.

What about children in content?

Agree in writing how children appear in content and where it can be used, and respect the family's privacy.
