How to find influencers for your brand.

Finding the right creator is the hardest part of influencer marketing. Here are five ways to do it, and what to check before you reach out.

By Karan Aryan, Founder, CreatorChahiye · Updated October 2026

In short

You can find influencers by searching manually on Instagram and YouTube, using hashtags and location tags, joining creator platforms, working with an agency or looking at who already talks about your category. Whichever route you take, vet audience fit and engagement quality before you reach out.

Start by defining who you need

Write down your customer, your city focus, your language and your goal. Then decide which tiers you want. Our guide to nano vs micro influencers can help.

Five ways to find creators

  1. Manual search. Search your category on Instagram and YouTube and look at who creates content for your customer. It is free but slow.
  2. Hashtags and location tags. Useful for finding local and nano creators who post about your category.
  3. Your own customers and followers. People who already tag or review your product are warm leads.
  4. Creator platforms and marketplaces. They give you a searchable database, but quality varies, so you still need to vet.
  5. An agency or network. An agency does discovery and vetting for you, for a fee. See how agencies charge.

Vet before you reach out

  • Does the audience match your customer in city, age and interests?
  • Are the comments genuine and relevant?
  • Do average views look healthy compared with followers?
  • Do past sponsored posts perform like normal posts?
  • Is the content style right for your product?

Our guide to choosing micro-influencers has the full checklist.

How to reach out

Send a short, personal message that explains the brand, the idea and what is on offer. Be clear about whether it is a gifted product or a paid collaboration. For seeding, read our product seeding guide.

Keep a simple tracker

Use a spreadsheet to track each creator, their contact, tier, audience notes, status and results. It saves time on the next campaign.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find influencers for my brand in India?

Search manually on Instagram and YouTube, use hashtags and location tags, look at your own customers, use creator platforms, or work with an agency. Vet audience fit and engagement before reaching out.

Where can I find micro-influencers?

Through manual search, hashtags, creator platforms and agencies. Check audience data and comments before you shortlist.

Should I use an influencer platform or an agency?

Platforms give you a searchable database and are cheaper but need more of your time. An agency handles discovery, vetting and coordination for a fee.

How many influencers should I shortlist?

Shortlist more than you need, then narrow down after checking audience fit and asking for insights.
