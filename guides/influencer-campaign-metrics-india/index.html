Influencer campaign metrics: what to track.

Likes and follower counts look good in a report but tell you little. Here is how to pick metrics that match your goal.

By Karan Aryan, Founder, CreatorChahiye · Updated October 2026

In short

Pick one goal first, then track the metrics that match it: reach and views for awareness, saves, shares and clicks for interest, and trackable links or codes for sales. Treat likes and follower count as context, not results. Review results two to four weeks after posting, then double down on what worked.

Start with one goal

Metrics only make sense against a goal. A launch campaign aimed at awareness should not be judged on sales in week one. Decide whether the main aim is awareness, interest or sales, and measure accordingly.

Metrics by goal

GoalMetrics to trackHow to measure
AwarenessViews, reach, sharesCreator's post insights
InterestSaves, comments that ask questions, profile visits, link clicksPost insights and tracked links
Sales or sign-upsOrders, sign-ups, cost per resultTracked links, discount codes, landing page
Content valuePerformance of the content as an adYour ad account results

Numbers to treat with caution

  • Follower count on its own
  • Likes, which are easy to inflate
  • Total impressions without context
  • Any number a creator or agency cannot back with a screenshot

How to make results trackable

  • Give each creator their own link with UTM tags: set utm_source to the platform, utm_medium to influencer, utm_campaign to the campaign name and utm_content to the creator, all in lowercase so the data groups cleanly
  • Or give each creator a unique discount code, and combine links and codes when you can
  • Send people to a dedicated landing page where possible
  • Ask creators for insight screenshots after posting
  • Note the posting date, so you can compare like with like

When to review

Check early numbers a few days after posting, but judge the campaign two to four weeks later, once views and sales have settled. Compare creators on cost per result, not on follower count. Use what you learn to run a bigger second round. Our startup playbook explains how to run a small pilot and scale it.

What a good report contains

A clear report lists each creator, what they posted, the main results against your goal, what worked and what did not, and a recommendation for the next step. Honest reporting includes the weak results too. That is how we write ours.

Frequently asked questions

What are the most important influencer marketing metrics?

It depends on your goal. For awareness, track views and reach. For interest, track saves, shares and link clicks. For sales, track orders using tracked links or discount codes.

How do I track sales from influencers?

Give each creator a unique link with UTM tags or a unique discount code, and send traffic to a dedicated landing page.

Are likes a good metric?

Likes are a weak signal on their own. Saves, shares, clicks and conversions say more about whether the content worked.

When should I measure results?

Look at early numbers within a few days, but judge the campaign two to four weeks after posting.
