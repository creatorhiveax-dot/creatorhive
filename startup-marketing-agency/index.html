  • One team, not five agencies. Creators, UGC, PR, scripts, performance ads and strategy under one plan

Frequently asked questions

What is a startup marketing agency?

An agency that plans and runs marketing for early-stage and growing startups, usually with smaller budgets, faster tests and a focus on what moves the next milestone. CreatorChahiye leads with influencer marketing and adds UGC, PR, performance ads and strategy.

Is CreatorChahiye startup-friendly?

Yes. Startups are who we are built for. Most begin with a small creator pilot instead of a retainer, and scale what works.

How much does startup marketing cost in India?

A creator pilot with nano and micro creators can start from around ₹25,000 to ₹50,000 in creator fees. PR starts from ₹50,000 and script writing from ₹5,000. Strategy and performance marketing are quoted on a call.

Do you work with funded startups?

Yes. After a round we help plan the order of spend: a pilot first, then scale creators, run creator content as ads, and pair it with PR for the announcement.

Do you only do influencer marketing?

Influencer marketing is our core, and we also run UGC, PR, script writing, celebrity PR, performance marketing and strategic management, so startups can keep marketing in one team.
