Why finance creators

People trust the person who explains money simply. Finance creators can turn complex ideas into short, clear content, which suits fintech apps, savings and education products. The same trust makes accuracy and disclosure especially important.

The rules you need to know

ASCI's influencer guidelines require clear disclosure on any promotional post, using labels such as Ad, Sponsored, Collaboration or Partnership. In August 2023, ASCI added extra expectations for finance and health influencers.

  • Creators giving investment advice are expected to show relevant registration or qualifications, for example SEBI registration for investment advice
  • For other financial advice, qualifications such as CA, CS or an insurance licence are expected, as applicable
  • These details should be disclosed prominently and upfront in the content
  • Breaking the rules can bring penalties under consumer protection law

Rules change. Check the current ASCI and SEBI guidance, and take your own compliance advice for your category.

Frequently asked questions

Do finance influencers need to be registered?

ASCI expects creators who give investment advice to show relevant registration, for example with SEBI, and other financial advisers to show relevant qualifications. Check the current ASCI and SEBI guidance for your case.

Can I work with finance creators for a fintech app?

Yes. Many fintech and finance brands work with creators for explainers and launches. The brief should avoid misleading claims and include clear disclosure.

Yes. Our network includes finance creators. Share your brief and we will shortlist the best fits.
