Why e-commerce brands use creators

Online shoppers cannot touch the product, so they look for someone who has. A creator showing the real size, colour and quality answers that better than a product photo. It also gives you a steady supply of content for listings and ads.

Own store or marketplace?

  • Own website: send creators to the product page with a tracked link or a creator-specific coupon code. You see clicks, orders and cost per order.
  • Marketplaces: link creators to your listing and give each one a unique code where the marketplace supports it. Reviews and ratings on the listing usually move as well.
  • Both: many brands use creators for the launch on their own store, then push marketplace sales during big sale events.

Sale seasons and scale

Festive sales and big marketplace sale events are when e-commerce brands want many creators live at once. Plan a few weeks ahead: shortlist and agree terms early, ship products in time, and keep posting dates tight around the sale. We run campaigns from a 5-creator pilot to 100+ creators going live together.

Frequently asked questions

Can creators drive sales on Amazon or Flipkart?

Yes. Creators link to your listing and, where the marketplace supports it, share a unique code. Track each creator separately so you can see who actually moves orders.

How many creators should an e-commerce brand start with?

Usually 5–10 nano and micro creators for a pilot. Scale up to more creators for a launch or sale event once you know which formats work.

Can we use creator videos on our product listing?

Yes, if usage rights are agreed in writing first. We include usage terms in every agreement.

What does it cost?

Creator fees depend on tier and format. See our cost guide. We add a transparent management fee and you always see what each creator is paid.
