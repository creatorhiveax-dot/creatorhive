How we run a UGC project

  1. We learn about your product, customer and where you want to use the content
  2. We shortlist creators whose style and audience fit your brand
  3. We write a short brief and agree format, length, revisions and deadline
  4. We agree usage rights: where the content can run, and for how long
  5. We manage approvals and delivery, and report back

Usage rights, agreed up front

Whether you use content organically, on your website or as paid ads changes the price. We agree this in the written agreement before any creator starts. See our influencer agreement checklist for what to cover.

Disclosure

If a creator is paid or gifted and posts on their own channel, the post should carry a clear disclosure label under ASCI's influencer guidelines. We build this into the agreement.

Frequently asked questions

What is UGC?

UGC is user-generated content. In practice, brands use the term for authentic-looking videos and photos made by real people or creators, which the brand then uses in its own channels and ads.

Do UGC creators need a big following?

Not necessarily. Because the content is used on your channels, quality and how natural it feels matter more than follower count.

Can I use UGC in paid ads?

Yes, if usage rights for paid ads are agreed in writing. Rights affect the price, so agree them before the creator starts.

How many creators should I start with?

Start small, with a few creators, so you can test different styles before scaling.
