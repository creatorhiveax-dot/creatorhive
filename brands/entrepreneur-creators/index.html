Why entrepreneur creators

Business audiences follow people whose advice they trust. An entrepreneur or business creator can show a tool in a real workflow, share a founder's view and explain why a product helps a business grow.

Content formats that work

  • Product walkthroughs in a real business use case
  • Founder stories and lessons
  • Short Reels with one practical tip
  • Comparisons of tools or services
  • Interviews and Q&A

What to check

  1. Does the audience include the kind of buyer you want, such as founders, freelancers or small business owners?
  2. Is the content practical, not just motivational?
  3. Do past sponsored posts feel honest?
  4. Is the creator comfortable with a clear disclosure label?

Frequently asked questions

Which brands suit entrepreneur creators?

B2B tools, SaaS, startup services and products aimed at small business owners and founders.

Do you have entrepreneur creators in your network?

Yes. Our network includes entrepreneurship and business creators. Tell us your product and we will shortlist the best fits.

How do I measure results for B2B?

Use tracked links, unique demo or sign-up pages and discount codes, and judge results over a longer window than for consumer products.

What does it cost?

Rates depend on the creator tier, format and usage rights. We share rates and audience data before you commit.
