Why D2C brands use creators

D2C brands win on trust, speed and content. Creators bring all three: an audience that already listens to them, content you can reuse in ads, and a fast way to test what message works. Our guide to influencer marketing for D2C brands in India explains the trends behind this.

What we do for D2C brands

  • Creator discovery. Shortlists built on niche, city, engagement quality and content style, never on follower count alone
  • Outreach and negotiation. We contact creators, agree rates and lock deliverables in writing
  • Campaign management. Briefs, drafts, approvals and posting schedules, with one point of contact
  • UGC and product seeding. See our guides on UGC and product seeding
  • Reporting. Reach, engagement and clicks in plain language

How a D2C campaign usually starts

  1. A discovery call to understand your product, audience and budget
  2. A creator shortlist with rates, engagement data and past work
  3. A small pilot with a few nano and micro creators
  4. A report, then a bigger second round on what worked

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with small D2C brands?

Yes. Startups and early-stage brands are who we are built for. Smaller budgets usually mean nano and micro creators, which often deliver better engagement per rupee.

How fast can a D2C campaign go live?

Typically one to two weeks from the discovery call to the first post, depending on creator availability and how quickly content is approved on your side.

What does it cost?

We charge a transparent management fee on top of creator costs, plus a minimal one-time creator shortlisting fee per campaign. You always see what creators are paid.

Can you track sales from creators?

We recommend tracked links or discount codes for each creator, so results can be measured. See our guide to campaign metrics.
