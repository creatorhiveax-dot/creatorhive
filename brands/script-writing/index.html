Pricing

Script writing starts from ₹5,000. The final price depends on the work: how many scripts, their length, how much research is needed, and the number of revisions. Packs of scripts for a campaign are priced together. You get a written quote before we start.

What makes a script work

  1. The hook. The first one to three seconds decide whether people keep watching
  2. One idea. A short video should make one point, clearly
  3. Show, then tell. Write for what is on screen, not just what is said
  4. One call to action. Tell viewers exactly what to do next
  5. Native to the platform. A Reel, a YouTube video and an ad need different pacing

Scripts are especially useful for UGC and creator campaigns. See also our guide on what UGC is.
