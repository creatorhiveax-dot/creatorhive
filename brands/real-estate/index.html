Why real estate brands use creators

A property is the biggest purchase most people make, and they want to see it through someone else's eyes first. Creators show the layout, the light, the commute and the neighbourhood, and they answer the questions buyers are shy to ask a sales team.

Formats that work

  • Property and show-flat walkthroughs
  • Neighbourhood guides: schools, metro, markets, weekends
  • "What your budget gets you" comparisons
  • Investor explainers on rental yield, payment plans and process
  • Site-visit and launch-event coverage

Which creators

Not only real estate creators. Finance and investing creators reach buyers thinking about returns; lifestyle and family creators in the same city reach end users; and NRI and expat creators reach buyers abroad, which matters for both Indian projects and Dubai property. See our finance creators.

Ad rules in India and Dubai

In India, an advertisement for a RERA-registered project is expected to show its RERA registration number, and that includes creator content. In Dubai, property advertising needs the right permit, and the UAE also has its own rules for paid influencer advertising. Rules change, so confirm the current requirements with your compliance or legal team; we build what they tell us into every brief and agreement, along with ASCI disclosure for Indian audiences.

Frequently asked questions

Do you run real estate campaigns in Dubai?

Yes. We run creator campaigns for property brands in India and Dubai, including creators who reach NRI and expat buyers. Permit and disclosure details are confirmed before content goes live.

Does creator content need the RERA number in India?

An advertisement for a RERA-registered project is expected to show its registration number, and creator posts count as advertising. We add it to the brief and check it before posting.

Which creators work best for property?

A mix: real estate and finance creators for investors, lifestyle and family creators in the city for end users, and NRI creators for buyers abroad.

How do you measure results?

Tracked links and creator-specific enquiry forms or codes, so site-visit requests and leads can be traced to each creator.
