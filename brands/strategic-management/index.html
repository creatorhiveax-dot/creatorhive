Strategic management for your brand.

Some brands do not need one more campaign. They need someone to decide what to run, where and in what order, and then keep it on track. That is what this service is.

What we handle

  • Positioning and messaging
  • Channel and budget plan
  • Creator and content calendar

In short

Strategic management is an on-demand service where we plan and manage your marketing direction: who you are talking to, which channels and creators to use, what to spend where, and what to change each month. Pricing depends on scope and is shared on a call.

What strategic management covers

  • Positioning and messaging. Who the customer is, what you want to be known for, and the lines every creator and ad should carry
  • Channel plan. Which mix of creators, UGC, PR and paid ads fits your stage and budget
  • Calendar. A month-by-month plan of launches, campaigns and content
  • Budget split. What to spend on creators, content and ads, and what to hold back for testing
  • Reviews. A regular look at what worked, what did not and what changes next month

Who it is for

  • Founders running marketing themselves who want a plan and someone accountable for it
  • Brands that have tried a few creator campaigns and want them to add up to something
  • Funded startups about to scale spend and wanting a clear order of priorities. See our startup guide

How it works

  1. A call to understand the business, numbers and goals
  2. A written plan: positioning, channels, calendar and budget
  3. Execution through our own services where it fits: creator campaigns, UGC, PR and performance marketing
  4. Monthly review and an updated plan

Pricing

How we work

  • A creator shortlist within a few days
  • Rates and audience data shared with you before anything starts
  • A written agreement covering deliverables, timelines, payments and usage rights
  • One point of contact for the whole campaign
  • Plain reporting, including what did not work

Frequently asked questions

What is the price of strategic management?

It depends on the scope, so we share it on a call. Call or message us on WhatsApp +91 70655 55395 to know more. You get a written quote before anything starts.

What is strategic management in marketing?

It is the planning and steering of your marketing: positioning, channels, calendar, budget split and regular reviews, so that individual campaigns work towards one goal.

Is this a monthly retainer?

It can be a one-time plan or an ongoing monthly engagement. We agree which on the call.

Do you also run the campaigns?

Yes, where it fits. Creator campaigns, UGC, PR, script writing and performance marketing can all be run by our team under the same plan.
