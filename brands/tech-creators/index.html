Why tech creators

Tech buyers research before they buy and often rely on creators who explain how a product really works. A good tech creator can show features, compare options and answer questions in the comments, which is difficult to do in a plain ad.

Content formats that work

  • Review and first-impression videos
  • Feature walkthroughs and tutorials
  • Comparisons and "which should you buy" videos
  • Short Reels explaining one feature or tip
  • Live demos and Q&A

What to check when choosing a tech creator

  1. Audience fit. Do their followers actually buy this kind of product, and in your target cities?
  2. Accuracy. Does the creator explain products correctly and fairly?
  3. Honest tone. Do they point out drawbacks? That builds trust in the review.
  4. Past sponsored posts. Do they perform like their regular content?
  5. Disclosure. Sponsored posts must carry a clear label, as per ASCI's guidelines.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have tech creators in your network?

Yes. Our creator network spans tech, finance, medical, entrepreneurship, fashion, lifestyle, fitness, food and entertainment. Tell us your product and we will shortlist the best fits.

Can a tech creator review my product honestly?

Yes, and honest reviews usually build more trust. Agree deliverables and the brief in writing, and avoid scripting every line.

Do I need to send the product in advance?

For hardware and apps, yes, so the creator can test it properly before filming. Agree timelines in the written agreement.

What does it cost?

Rates depend on the creator tier, format and usage rights. We share rates and audience data before you commit.
