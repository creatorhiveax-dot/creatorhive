What our PR work covers

  • Story and angle. Finding the news in what you are doing: a launch, funding, a milestone or a founder story
  • Press release writing. A clear, factual release in the format journalists expect
  • Media pitching. Personalised pitches to relevant journalists, newsletters and publications for your sector
  • Founder profiles and thought leadership. Articles and talking points that build credibility
  • Launch PR. Coordinating media, creators and social around a launch date

Pricing

PR engagements start from ₹50,000. The final price depends on the scope: one announcement or an ongoing programme, the number of outlets and markets, and how much writing is involved. You get a written quote before anything starts. Read how agency fees usually work in our guide to agency fees in India.

How a PR engagement works

  1. A call to understand the story, audience and goal
  2. An angle, a media list and a timeline, agreed in writing
  3. Writing the press release or story, with your approval
  4. Pitching and follow-up
  5. A short report of who was pitched, responses and coverage

PR with creators

PR and creator marketing work well together. A launch can combine media coverage with creator campaigns and scripted Reels, so the same story reaches people in more than one place.

Frequently asked questions

How much do PR services cost in India?

Our PR engagements start from ₹50,000. The final price depends on scope, such as a single announcement or an ongoing programme, the number of outlets and the writing involved. You get a written quote first.

Do you guarantee media coverage?

No. Journalists decide what they cover, so no honest PR team can guarantee coverage. We focus on a strong angle, a well-written release and targeted pitching to improve the chances.

What do I need to start?

A short brief: what you want to announce, why it matters, any numbers you can share, and founder details. We help shape the story from there.

How long does a PR campaign take?

A single announcement usually takes a few weeks from brief to pitching. Ongoing PR runs month to month, as agreed in writing.

Can you combine PR with influencer marketing?

Yes. Many launches combine media outreach with creator content, which we can run together.
