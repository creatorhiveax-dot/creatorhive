Services

What we take off your plate

01

Creator discovery

Shortlists built on audience fit — niche, city, engagement quality and content style. Never vanity follower counts.

02

Outreach & negotiation

We contact creators, negotiate fair rates, and lock deliverables, timelines and usage rights in writing before anything starts.

03

Campaign management

Briefs, drafts, approvals, posting schedules — all handled. One point of contact instead of twenty creator chats.

04

UGC & product seeding

Authentic content for your ads and pages, plus seeding programs that turn creators into long-term advocates.

05

Honest reporting

Reach, engagement and clicks in plain language. No inflated numbers — and we'll tell you what didn't work.

06

Written agreements

Every campaign runs on a clear agreement. The brand's budget is protected; so is the creator's payment.

Process

A free 30-minute call to understand your product, audience and budget. If influencer marketing isn't right for you yet, we'll say so.

02

Creator shortlist

Within days you get a curated shortlist — creators matched to your audience with rates, engagement data and past work.

03

Campaign launch

Briefs, agreements, content approvals and posting schedules handled end to end, visible in one shared tracker.

04

Report & scale

A clear performance report with honest recommendations on what to double down on next.
