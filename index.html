Questions we get every week

What does CreatorChahiye do?

CreatorChahiye is an influencer marketing agency in India for startups and D2C brands. We handle creator discovery, outreach and negotiation, campaign management, UGC, product seeding and reporting, and every campaign runs on a written agreement. See our services.

Who do you work with?

Startups, D2C and growing brands that want results without an in-house influencer team, and creators from nano to macro who want fair, paid collaborations. Our network spans fashion, lifestyle, travel, tech, finance, medical, parenting, fitness and more.

How much does influencer marketing cost in India?

Meaningful micro-influencer campaigns in India can start from around ₹25,000 to ₹50,000 in creator fees. We charge a transparent management fee on top of creator costs, plus a minimal one-time creator shortlisting fee per campaign, and you always see what creators are paid. Read our cost guide.

How fast can a campaign go live?

Typically one to two weeks from the discovery call to the first post, depending on creator availability and how quickly content is approved on your side.

Is CreatorChahiye a registered business?

Yes. CreatorChahiye (formerly CreatorHive) is GST registered and MSME (Udyam) registered, and every campaign runs on a written agreement covering deliverables, timelines, payments and usage rights.

How do creators join?

Creators register once for free on our Join as Creator page. When a brand campaign matches their niche and audience, we reach out. There is no fee and no lock-in. Join as Creator.
