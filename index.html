Questions

Answered honestly.

What budget do I need to start?

Meaningful micro-influencer campaigns in India can start from around ₹25,000–₹50,000 in creator fees. On the call we'll tell you honestly what your budget can and cannot achieve — before you spend anything.

How do you choose creators?

Audience fit first: who actually follows the creator, how they engage, and whether they match your customer. Follower count comes last. We also check for fake engagement before shortlisting anyone.

What do you charge?

A transparent management fee on top of creator costs, plus a minimal one-time creator shortlisting fee per campaign — covering the manual sourcing and authenticity vetting behind every list. You always see exactly what creators are paid.

Do you work with formal agreements?

Yes — and we consider it a feature, not paperwork. Every campaign runs on a clear written agreement covering deliverables, timelines, payments and usage rights. Brands know their budget is protected; creators know their payment is.

Do you work with small or new brands?

Yes — startups and early-stage brands are exactly who we're built for. Smaller budgets usually mean micro and nano creators, which often deliver better engagement per rupee.

How fast can a campaign go live?

Typically 1–2 weeks from the discovery call to the first post, depending on creator availability and how quickly content gets approved on your side.
